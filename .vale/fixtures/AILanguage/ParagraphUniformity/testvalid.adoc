The procurement team reviewed the vendor contract on Monday and found several clauses that needed a rewrite before the finance group would agree to sign the final agreement.

Legal confirmed the concern.

The finance group met on Wednesday to weigh the revised terms and decided, after a long discussion that ran well past the scheduled hour and drew in two directors who had not read the original draft, that the updated language protected the company well enough to move ahead with the signing despite the lingering doubts of one reviewer.

The vendor signed on Thursday.
